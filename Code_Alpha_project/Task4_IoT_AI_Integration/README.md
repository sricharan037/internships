# Task 4: Mini Project – IoT Case Study 

## Topic
**Integration of IoT and Artificial Intelligence: Transforming Industries and Smart Systems**

## Overview
This comprehensive case study explores how the combination of the **Internet of Things (IoT)** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**—often referred to as AIoT—is revolutionizing various sectors. It analyzes the synergy between massive IoT sensor networks and intelligent AI algorithms, detailing real-world applications, benefits, operational challenges, and future prospects.

## Key Sections Covered
1. **Introduction**: Defining AIoT and exploring why combining data collection with intelligent analysis is highly effective.
2. **How IoT and AI Work Together**: The interaction between massive real-time data streams and predictive algorithms (comparing Edge AI vs. Cloud AI).
3. **Major Applications**:
   - Predictive Maintenance in Industrial sectors
   - Smart Healthcare (Remote Patient Monitoring)
   - Smart Agriculture (Crop Monitoring & Yield Prediction)
   - Autonomous Vehicles & Smart Traffic Management
   - Energy Management and Smart Grids
4. **Real-World Case Studies**:
   - GE’s Predix Platform (Industrial AI)
   - IBM Watson IoT
   - Tesla’s Autonomous Driving Fleet
   - Smart Farming deployments in India
5. **Benefits**: Higher accuracy, operational efficiency, reduced downtime, and personalized real-time decision making.
6. **Challenges**: Data security and privacy, computational requirements at the edge, integration complexity, and the industry skills gap.
7. **Future Scope**: The role of 5G, Edge Computing, Digital Twins, and the technological vision for 2030.
